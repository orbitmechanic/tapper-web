How to tend the bar

Patrons walk in from the doors and advance on your kegs. Pour a mug and it slides down the lane to the first customer who can catch it. A catch pushes them back; if they clear the door, they are served.

Do not drop the round

Empty mugs come back to the keg and must be caught, and a full mug that reaches the door breaks. Three mistakes end the shift. Tips trigger a short dancing distraction and pay 1,500.

Four bars, one night

Thirteen screens across the saloon, sports bar, punk bar and alien bar, then loop with faster crowds. Clear a theme to play the shell game: one can is still, five are shaken.
